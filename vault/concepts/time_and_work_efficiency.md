# Time & Work: The Unitary & Efficiency Method

**Subject**: Quantitative Aptitude  
**Topic**: [[Arithmetic]]  
**Difficulty**: Level 1 to Level 2  
**Target Time**: 35-45s  

---

## 1. Core Mathematical Principle
$$\text{Total Work} = \text{Total Days} \times \text{Efficiency (Work done per day)}$$

- When multiple persons work together:
$$\text{Net Efficiency} = E_A + E_B + E_C$$
$$\text{Total Time Taken} = \frac{\text{Total Work (LCM of individual days)}}{\text{Net Efficiency}}$$

---

## 2. Standard 80/20 Shortcut Patterns
1. **Two Workers Formula**:
   If A takes $x$ days and B takes $y$ days:
   $$\text{Combined Time} = \frac{x \cdot y}{x + y}$$
2. **Alternate Day Work**:
   Always calculate work done in complete 2-day cycles before tackling remaining fraction.
