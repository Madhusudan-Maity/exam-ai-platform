# Exam Trap: km/h vs m/s Unit Conversion

**Category**: [[Trap]] / Calculation Slip  
**Trigger Exams**: SSC CPO, WBP Constable, WBP SI, SSC JE  

---

## The Trap
Questions give the train length in **meters** and time in **seconds**, but options are in **$\text{km/h}$** (or vice-versa). 

### The Conversion Multiplier:
- From $\text{km/h} \longrightarrow \text{m/s}$: Multiply by $\frac{5}{18}$
- From $\text{m/s} \longrightarrow \text{km/h}$: Multiply by $\frac{18}{5}$

### Rule to Remember:
- Going to **S**maller unit ($\text{m/s}$): **S**maller number on top ($\frac{5}{18}$).
- Going to **B**igger unit ($\text{km/h}$): **B**igger number on top ($\frac{18}{5}$).
